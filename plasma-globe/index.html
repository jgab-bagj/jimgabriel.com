Overview

Inside Plasma Globe: Fidget Lamp

Plasma Globe puts a classic plasma ball under your fingers. Touch the glass and lightning leaps from the coil to meet you, one bolt for every finger.

Built to feel alive on a desk or in your hands, Plasma Globe pairs real multitouch lightning with responsive haptics, subtle tilt parallax, and rich night-room ambience. Leave it untouched and it settles into a calm, glowing desk lamp.

Real Multitouch Lightning

Every finger draws its own crackling filament — up to five simultaneous bolts on iPhone and even more on iPad.

Tactile Haptics & Sound

Feel a low electric hum and sharp snaps on iPhone as arcs jump to your fingertips, paired with reactive crackle and night-time ambience of crickets, crows, and the occasional cat.

Ten Colors & Spectrum Tube

Pick from ten vivid lightning presets or sweep continuously across the spectrum tube to tune any wavelength.

Atmosphere & Desk Lamp Mode

Tilt your device to peer around the glass sphere amid drifting fog, a swinging lamp, and moving shadows. The screen stays awake, settling into a calm glow when left untouched.

Tip for iPad Users Four- and five-finger swipes belong to iPadOS system gestures. To rest your whole hand flat on the glass, disable them in Settings → Multitasking & Gestures.
Visual Sensitivity Note Contains flickering and flashing light effects.
