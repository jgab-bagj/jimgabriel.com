Overview

Inside School Time!

School Time is a living reef aquarium you can touch, and a timer you can watch.

Sixteen hundred golden fish swim as one across a sunlit coral reef. Lead them with a finger, split the school with two hands, or let them roam freely on a second screen. When it's time to focus, set any timer from 30 seconds to 2 hours and press HUNT — a pack of barracuda drops in and catches the fish at the exact pace of your countdown.

Lead 1,600 Golden Fish

Touch the glass and the entire school follows your finger. Spread two fingers to split the school in two, bring them together to merge, or tap to startle them.

The Barracuda Countdown

Set any timer from 0:30 to 2:00:00 and press HUNT. One glance tells you how much time remains: it's the size of the school. When the last fish disappears in a puff of bubbles, a buoy bell rings.

Four Living Reef Views

Explore corals, sea fans, sponges, clownfish, tangs, parrotfish, and nautiluses from four camera perspectives — in the tank, gallery window, down the reef, or swimming with the school — each with pan and zoom.

Built for Focus & Everyday Timers

Keeps ticking in the background with optional finish notifications, subtle sonar pings, pause/resume controls, and an automatic power saver for long sessions.

Questions & Help

Does the timer keep running if I lock my screen or switch apps?
Yes. The timer stays accurate in the background and sends a notification when time is up if notifications are allowed.
Is it kid-friendly?
Yes — rated 4+ with no blood or gore. When a barracuda catches a fish, it disappears in a puff of bubbles.
Can I just watch the aquarium without setting a timer?
Absolutely. Lift your hand and the school roams naturally across the reef, making it great to leave running on an iPad or second screen.
