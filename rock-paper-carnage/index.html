Overview

Inside Rock, Paper, Carnage

You were on your couch. Now you're a contestant on the galaxy's most violent game show, and the only thing you brought is your hand.

Rock, Paper, Carnage is rock, paper, scissors with finishing moves. Win the throw, punch in the combo before the clock runs out, and your hand turns into a boulder, a buzzsaw, a storm of paper cuts, and more. Miss it and the crowd boos. Lose the round and your opponent finishes you.

Read Your Opponent

Every opponent has a tell — and most of the time it tells the truth. Spot it, throw what beats it, and earn that level's signature finisher.

Rhythm Showdowns

Throw the same sign and your hands lock into a rhythm duel set to an original song. Win a showdown for a star; two stars unlock your Ultimate.

Eight Opponents, Eight Arenas

Face a baby monkey under the circus big top, a toddler at daycare, an orangutan in the rainforest canopy, a coworker in the office break room, a bodybuilder at the beach gym, a silverback in the aquarium hall, the Galactic Warlord, and the Kraken.

Unlock More Carnage

Earn points on the reward track to unlock finishers like the Wrecking Ball, the Guillotine, and the Meteor — plus blood styles from slime to confetti. Beat a boss to unlock Hard Mode.

Support & Privacy

Questions & Help

Where is my progress saved?
All game progress is saved locally on your device. Deleting the app removes your saved progress.
Does the game require an internet connection?
No. Rock, Paper, Carnage plays completely offline.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. It is a single $0.99 one-time purchase with zero ads and zero in-app purchases.
Why does iOS mention the camera or microphone in diagnostics?
That reference comes from code bundled inside the game engine's video player. Rock, Paper, Carnage never accesses your camera or microphone.
